# Receipt and Photo Capture — final plan (with your refinements)

## What you get
- An optional "Add photo" button on four screens: Paper Sales, Cash Taken Out, Purchases, Supplier Payment.
- Photos are shrunk on the phone (about 200 KB) before sending, so they upload fast on market data.
- Saving a sale, payout or purchase never waits for the photo. A photo can also be added later.
- Owners see the photos (tap to enlarge) when reviewing; accountant exports show "receipt attached" and a count.

## Rules we keep
- Photos live in a private folder. No public links are ever saved; each view gets a link that expires after 1 hour.
- Every photo belongs to one business. Staff can only see and add photos for their own business.
- Photos cannot be edited. An owner can mark one as "not valid" with a reason (soft delete); it stays in the history and audit log but no longer counts.
- Many photos per record are allowed.
- Only images (JPEG, PNG, WebP), 10 MB maximum before shrinking. Location data in the photo is removed by the shrinking step.

## Who can add photos (first release)
| Screen | Who can add |
| --- | --- |
| Paper Sales | cashier who entered it, owner, Supa Admin |
| Cash Taken Out | person who recorded it, owner, Supa Admin |
| Purchases | purchaser, owner, Supa Admin |
| Supplier Payment | purchaser, owner, Supa Admin |
Only owner and Supa Admin can mark a photo not valid.

## Technical details
- Private bucket `receipts`; path `<business_id>/<record_type>/<record_id>/<uuid>.jpg`, generated by the app, never typed by the user.
- Table `receipts`: business_id, record_type (purchase | payout | supplier_payment | late_entry), record_id, storage_path unique, file_size_bytes, mime_type, width_px, height_px, caption ≤255, uploaded_by, uploaded_at, deleted_at, deleted_by, delete_reason. Index on (business_id, record_type, record_id). Table comment states many-per-record and soft-delete rule.
- Change from the uploaded draft: the browser does NOT insert rows directly. A database function `attach_receipt(...)` checks that the record exists, belongs to the caller's business, and that the caller's role is allowed for that record type, then saves the row and writes the audit log. `void_receipt(id, reason≥5)` is owner/Supa Admin only. No direct insert/update/delete grants; reads via a scoped function or business-scoped read policy; visitors get nothing.
- Storage policies: folder 1 must equal the caller's business_id from their login, plus a role check; no update; delete only for owner/Supa Admin (files are kept by default on soft delete).
- An upload whose row is not saved is cleaned up by the app; leftover files without a row are reported in a health check, never silently deleted.
- Shrinking: canvas re-encode, longest side 1600 px, JPEG quality 0.8 (also strips photo metadata).
